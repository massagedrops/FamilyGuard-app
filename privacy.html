Privacy Policy

FamilyGuard · Last updated: 19 November 2026

FamilyGuard ("the App", "we", "us") is a family parental-control service made up of two apps: a parent app and a child-supervision app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

FamilyGuard is intended only for parents or legal guardians to supervise their own minor children's devices, with the child's and family's knowledge. It must never be used to monitor another adult without that person's clear consent.

1. Who sets up monitoring

A parent or legal guardian creates a pairing code on their own device, then installs the child-supervision app on a device belonging to their own minor child and enters that code to link the two devices. Monitoring only begins after this deliberate pairing step.

2. Information we collect from the child device

Once paired, and only as permitted by settings the parent turns on, the child app may share the following with the paired parent's account:

  • Installed app list, app usage time and app lock/limit status
  • Websites visited in supported browsers, for the purpose of filtering unsafe content
  • Device status: battery level, online/offline state, basic device name
  • Notifications received on the device (excluding banking/payment/SMS one-time-code notifications, which are never shared)
  • Live camera snapshot, one-way audio, and screen-mirroring, only when the parent actively starts a session and only while that session is running. Android shows its own system camera/microphone/screen-recording indicators on the child device whenever any of these are active, and this cannot be hidden by the app.
  • SOS alerts the child chooses to send

3. How information is used

Data collected is used solely to show the paired parent a dashboard of their child's device activity and status, and to let the parent apply app locks, screen-time limits, and website filtering. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where data is stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore, Realtime Database, and Firebase Authentication), under Google's own security and data-processing terms. Access is limited to the paired parent account for each child device.

5. Data retention and deletion

A parent can disconnect a child device at any time from the app, which stops further data collection from that device. To request deletion of previously collected data for your family, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a tool for parents/guardians to supervise their own children's devices; it is not directed at children as independent users, and a child does not create their own account within the App.

7. Permissions used by the child-supervision app

The child-supervision app (installed separately on the child's own device, outside app stores) requests the device permissions needed for the features above — such as camera, microphone, accessibility service, notification access, and device-admin — and uses them only for the parental-supervision features described in this policy.

8. Your choices

  • A parent may disconnect/unpair a device at any time.
  • A parent may turn off any individual feature (e.g. notification sharing, web history) in the app's settings.
  • Families may request a copy or deletion of their data by contacting us.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact: massagedrops5@gmail.com

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
